Security

Your work deserves thoughtful protection.

CareDraft's note-drafting AI runs on your device, and saved records are encrypted locally. Clear boundaries help you understand what stays on your phone and what happens when you choose connected tools.

AES-256-GCM at rest

Every note, transcript, and recording is encrypted on the device with authenticated encryption.

Hardened key derivation

Your PIN protects the encryption key through PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 key derivation.

Biometric unlock

Open with fingerprint or face using your phone's secure hardware. The PIN stays as the root of trust.

Offline recovery code

One recovery code, generated on your device, shown once. It's the only spare key — and it's in your pocket, not on our servers.

No account for note drafting

No account for note drafting and no advertising trackers. CareDraft does not operate a clinical-content backend. Android photo-to-text processes images on-device; its Google ML Kit SDK reports technical usage and diagnostics to Google.

Clear connection boundaries

Model downloads and address suggestions use the network. Your speech provider may process dictation online; exports and navigation go where you choose.

Plain talk

A few things to know before you start.

AI drafts need your eyes

Speech models can mishear numbers, sides, and medication names, and language models can make confident-sounding mistakes. Every draft is labeled as a draft, and reviewing it is part of the job — yours.

Built for de-identified drafting

CareDraft is a personal drafting tool for de-identified, post-encounter notes. Avoid patient identifiers, and follow your organization's documentation, privacy, and device policies.

No cloud backup, on purpose

CareDraft does not keep a server backup of your notes. If you lose both your PIN and your recovery code, the data is gone for good — no support ticket can bring it back. That's the cost of nobody else holding a key.

Needs a modern phone

Local AI needs available storage and memory. CareDraft E2B starts at 8 GB phone RAM with a roughly 3.4 GB model download. After setup, the drafting model can run offline.

Questions

A little more about CareDraft.

What works offline?

After the model download, AI note drafting runs offline. You can also type notes and use local records without a connection. Offline dictation depends on your phone having offline speech recognition available. Address suggestions and external map services need their own connections.

Does my dictation go to the cloud?

CareDraft uses your device's configured speech-recognition service. With offline recognition available, it can transcribe locally; on Android, the selected provider may use network processing depending on your settings. CareDraft LLC does not receive the audio or transcript. The CareDraft model drafts the note on your phone.

Is CareDraft HIPAA compliant?

CareDraft is intended for de-identified, post-encounter drafting. Local processing and encryption do not by themselves establish that a workflow meets your organization's requirements. Check with your organization before using it for clinical work, and read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Which phones does it support?

CareDraft is in field testing on modern Android phones. Local AI is demanding — recent mid-range and flagship devices work best. An iOS release is planned.

What makes the CareDraft AI model custom?

CareDraft E2B and E4B are our own fine-tuned versions of Google's Gemma models. Fine-tuning means giving an AI additional training for a specific job — in this case, organizing home-health dictation into clinical notes. They run on your phone after download. This focused training does not guarantee accuracy; every draft still needs clinician review.

Will the CareDraft AI model continue to improve?

Yes. We will continue updating CareDraft's AI models as technology improves, with a focus on note quality, reliability, and performance on your phone. Every draft will still need your review and approval.

What data was used to train CareDraft AI?

Our downloadable models were trained on synthetic examples: fictional home-health visit dictations paired with example notes. These cover physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, and skilled nursing. Your notes and dictation are not collected to train these models.

What if I forget my PIN?

Use the recovery code you saved at setup to unlock and set a new PIN. If you lose both the PIN and the recovery code, your data is unrecoverable — we hold no keys and no copies, so there's no master unlock. Keep that code somewhere safe.

CareDraft background dictation demonstration

CareDraft 1.0.1 (24), recorded on a physical Android phone. All transcript content is fictional. The user starts dictation while CareDraft is visible. Recording continues after navigation and while another app is open. An ongoing recording notification has a Stop action.

Open the video directly

This silent video contains chronological excerpts. Android Settings and notification views are cropped to exclude personal account details and unrelated notifications. After notification Stop, CareDraft auto-locks; the final excerpt shows the retained fictional transcript reopened from Work Queue after unlocking. Waiting and unlocking are omitted.

Android dictation uses the selected platform speech provider. This demonstration verifies recording controls and transcript retention; it does not validate clinical accuracy.
